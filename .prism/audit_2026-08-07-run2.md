## Forward-Compatibility Risk (found during Phase 5 verification, not a current bug)
- `modules/datetime_intel.py:62` — `detect_gaps()` calls `pd.Timedelta(pd.tseries.frequencies.to_offset(freq))`.
  This works for fixed-length frequencies (Day, Hour, ...) but raises `ValueError` for
  calendar-relative offsets (BusinessDay, MonthEnd, ...) since pandas 3.0's stricter
  `Timedelta` constructor rejects non-fixed-duration offsets. Currently harmless because
  the only caller (app.py:936) always passes the default `freq="D"`, and requirements.txt
  pins pandas==2.3.3. Flagged for a future run: (1) if `detect_gaps` is ever called with an
  inferred freq (e.g. business-day stock data), or (2) whenever the pandas pin moves to 3.x.
  Fix: catch the ValueError and fall back to a business-day-aware step, or use
  `full_range.freq.nanos` where available.
- Also noted: `google.generativeai` (the pinned Gemini SDK) is fully deprecated upstream in
  favor of `google.genai` — a FutureWarning fires on every app start. Worth a dedicated
  future-run migration (bigger than a "small fix alongside features" — new client init
  pattern, different streaming API).
