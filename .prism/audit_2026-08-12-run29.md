**One real gap found:** `modules/auto_insights.py`'s correlation detector
(`_detect_correlation_insights`) reports a bare point-estate Pearson r for
every flagged pair with no uncertainty quantification — a strong r on a
20-row sample and a strong r on a 20,000-row sample render identically
("strongly correlated, r=0.87") even though the former is far less
trustworthy. This is the one auto-surfaced (zero-click) insight category in
the app that ships a number with no confidence signal at all — everywhere
else numeric findings get one eventually (Hypothesis Sweep's post-hoc power
badges, Runs 25-28). Selected as this run's feature; see
`research_2026-08-12-run29.md`.

**Second gap (logged, not built this run):** `modules/mllab.py`'s
`run_baseline_models()` evaluates on a single 80/20 train/test split with no
cross-validation — a single split gives no sense of metric variance, and
"did you use cross-validation?" is one of the most common data-scientist
interview screening questions (confirmed by this run's Phase 2 research).
Logged as next run's strongest backlog candidate.

consistent with 5+ consecutive prior audits.
